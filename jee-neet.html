JEE and NEET coaching, built on concepts.

Two entrance programs with experienced faculty, regular testing and personal feedback.

Engineering

JEE Main & Advanced

Our JEE program covers Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics in depth, with a focus on how questions are actually asked. Students learn the concept, practise it at three difficulty levels and then test it under timed conditions.

Enquire about JEE

  • Two-year program alongside Class 11 and 12, plus a one-year option for Class 12 and repeaters.
  • Problem-solving sessions separate from theory classes.
  • Chapter, part and full syllabus tests in JEE Main and Advanced formats.
  • Detailed test analysis with a personal plan for each student.
Medical

NEET UG

NEET is won on Biology and accuracy. We teach Biology line by line from NCERT, strengthen Physics and Chemistry numericals, and train students to stay calm and accurate through a three-hour paper.

Enquire about NEET

  • NCERT-first Biology with diagrams, tables and regular recall tests.
  • Physics and Chemistry taught from basic to NEET level.
  • Weekly mock tests in the NEET pattern with rank analysis.
  • Mentoring for time management and exam temperament.
Good to know

Questions we hear most

Can my child join in Class 11 and continue to Class 12?

Yes. Most JEE and NEET students join in Class 11 and stay through Class 12. Seats in the senior batches are limited, so we suggest enquiring early.

Is school syllabus covered along with the entrance preparation?

Yes. Board and entrance topics are taught together, so students don't need a separate board tuition.

Do you offer a one-year or repeater batch?

Yes. Contact us for the current batch schedule and eligibility.

How often are tests held?

Weekly chapter tests, monthly cumulative tests and full-length mocks closer to the exam.
