Who we are

An academy built around one idea: a student who understands never needs to cram.

Advent Academy was started to give students from Class 8 to 12 a place where teaching is unhurried, clear and honest. We prepare students for school exams, the CBSE and state boards, and for the JEE and NEET entrance tests.

We run our own classrooms, equipped with smart-learning technology, so every lesson starts on time and in a space designed for learning. Our teachers plan together across classes, so a student moving from Class 10 to Class 11 meets a familiar approach, not a sudden jump.

Our name means "arrival". We hope each student arrives at their class, their exam and their career prepared and sure of themselves.
